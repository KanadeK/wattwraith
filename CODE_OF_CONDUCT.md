# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience for
everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics,
gender identity and expression, experience, education, socioeconomic status,
nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, color, religion, or sexual identity
and orientation.

## Expected behavior

Be respectful, accept constructive feedback, focus on the work, and protect private
data shared during security or bug reports. Harassment, intimidation, deliberate
misinformation, and publication of another person's private information are not
acceptable.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behavior through the repository's private security contact.
Maintainers may remove, edit, or reject contributions or comments that violate these
standards and may temporarily or permanently ban participants for harmful conduct.

This policy is adapted from Contributor Covenant 2.1.
